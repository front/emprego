About Frontkom

Frontkom is a digital consultancy agency based in Norway that specializes in developing and implementing open-source solutions.

Frontkom has an expanding team and is recruiting a Mid/Senior Full Stack Laravel Developer to work at the Funchal office, or remotely from Portugal.

The selected candidate will primarily be responsible for building web solutions with Laravel, working on medium to high-complexity projects with a focus on scalability and maintainability.

Profile

  • Ease of communication (spoken and written).
  • Comfortable interacting with both technical and non-technical clients.
  • A strong sense of responsibility and a good team player.
  • Well-organized and good at managing your time.
  • Curious and eager to learn new technologies.
  • Willingness to travel abroad occasionally.

Skills

Requirements

  • Minimum educational qualifications: High-school (12th year).
  • Good knowledge of English (spoken and written).
  • Proven experience with Laravel and its ecosystem, including Eloquent, queues, and broadcasting.
  • Strong understanding of SOLID principles and experience applying Domain-Driven Design (DDD).
  • PHP development experience, using the latest features of PHP 8 and MySQL/MariaDB.
  • Experience with frontend frameworks such as Vue.js, React, or Livewire.
  • Familiarity with component-based UIs and experience building reusable UI components.

Valued skills

  • Higher education in Computer Engineering or equivalent.
  • Experience with Pest and PHPUnit for testing.
  • Experience with frontend build tools such as Laravel Mix, Webpack, or Vite.
  • Familiarity with API documentation using tools like Postman or Swagger.
